## Sign in with an email and password
Open the sign-in screen, enter an email and a password, press **Sign in** — you land on the home screen.

## A wrong password says so
Enter a wrong password — the form says the password is wrong and keeps the email you typed.

## Forgot password sends an email
Press **Forgot password?** and enter your email — a message says the reset email is on its way.

## Sign out returns to sign-in
Sign out from the account menu — you are back on the sign-in screen.

## Also worth checking
- The email field keeps what you typed after a failed attempt
- Pressing Enter in the password field signs in
